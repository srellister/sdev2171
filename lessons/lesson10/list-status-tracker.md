Use this tracker at the midpoint or checkpoint if your instructor asks for a quick status update.

## Status options
- `configured`
  The list renders and the empty-state message appears correctly.
- `partial`
  The list or empty-state flow exists, but one part still fails.
- `blocked`
  The list-rendering flow cannot be completed during class without recovery support.
